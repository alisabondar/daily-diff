# Taste profile (grown by /fmk). Copy to ~/.claude/taste.md: the sandbox couldn't write there.

- Readability first: kills anything "hard to read" or a "wall of text". (Daily Diff, 2026-11-03)
- Wants character, but hates corporate/admin and Jira looks. (Daily Diff)
- Allergic to gimmicks: bubbles, decorative stat bars, cheesy gradients. (Daily Diff)
- Likes editorial/newspaper hierarchy: serif headlines, a clear lead, numbered priorities. (Daily Diff)
- Group by topic, not by time; ranking beats chronology. (Daily Diff)
- "Busy" is a kill word: mixed systems on one screen (tiles plus lists) die. (Daily Diff)
- Calm over loud: a soft muted palette (sage/beige, desaturated accents) and a humanist sans (Optima) beat high-contrast fashion, brutalist and broadsheet looks. (Daily Diff, round 6)
- Likes a warm human sign-off and quiet closure ("That's all. Go make something."). (Daily Diff)
- Passed over habit gimmicks (streaks, progress bars, "picked because"); prefers the content to be the reason to return. (Daily Diff, round 4)
